# Hard Thrust - Back Matter

Use these pages at the end of your formatted manuscript, after the final chapter/epilogue.

---

## Page 1: Acknowledgments (Optional)

```
ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

[Thank people who helped with the book: beta readers, editors, cover designer, family, etc.]

Example:
This book wouldn't exist without [names]. Thanks to [editor name] for catching my mistakes, [beta readers] for their honest feedback, and [family/friends] for putting up with me during the writing process.

Any remaining errors are entirely my own.
```

*(Skip if you prefer not to include. Common in fiction but not required.)*

---

## Page 2: About the Author

```
ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Joshua Cox writes dark romance and psychological thrillers that explore the space between love and obsession. When he's not crafting morally gray characters and devastating plot twists, he [hobby/location/personal detail].

Hard Thrust is his [first/debut] novel.

[Optional: Connect with Joshua at website.com or on social media @handle]
```

**CUSTOMIZE THIS** - Add your own details:
- Where you're based (optional)
- What you do when not writing
- Other books (if any)
- Website/social media (if you want readers to find you)

**Alternative versions:**

*Shorter (for eBook):*
```
Joshua Cox writes dark romance and psychological thrillers. Hard Thrust is his debut novel.
```

*Genre-focused:*
```
Joshua Cox believes love stories should be complicated, morally ambiguous, and impossible to put down. He writes psychological thrillers with romance at their dark heart. Hard Thrust is his first novel.
```

## Page 4: Reader Request (Highly Recommended)

```
A NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

Thank you for reading Hard Thrust. If you enjoyed this book, please consider leaving a review on Amazon. Reviews help other readers discover new books and mean the world to independent authors.

Even a sentence or two makes a difference.

Thank you for your support.

—Joshua
```

*(Reviews are crucial for visibility. This gentle ask works.)*

## Back Matter Order (Recommended)

1. Acknowledgments (if including)
2. About the Author
3. Also By (if applicable)
4. Reader Request (review ask)
5. Newsletter/Connect (if applicable)
6. Preview (if applicable)

---

## Notes

- **eBook**: Include all of the above that apply; readers often flip to back matter
- **Print**: Same content; About the Author is standard, review request is smart
- **Keep it short**: Back matter should be quick to read
- **Update later**: You can revise back matter in future editions as you build your platform
