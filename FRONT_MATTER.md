# Hard Thrust - Front Matter

---

## Page 1: Half Title (optional)

```

```

*(Just the title, centered, nothing else. Often omitted in ebooks.)*

## Page 6: Content Warning (Recommended for this genre)

```

This novel contains explicit sexual content, violence, psychological trauma, serious illness, and themes of grief, memory loss, and moral ambiguity.

Reader discretion advised.
```

*(Dark romance readers expect and appreciate content warnings. Language kept vague to avoid spoiling reveals while still warning sensitive readers.)*

## Notes on Front Matter

1. **eBook**: Can simplify—often just title page, copyright, content warning, then Chapter 1
2. **Print**: Include half title for professional look; ensure Chapter 1 starts on right-hand (odd) page
3. **Table of Contents**: Optional for fiction. Kindle Create auto-generates one for eBooks.
4. **Copyright year**: Use the year of first publication (2026)
5. **ISBN**: Leave blank until KDP assigns it, or insert if you purchase your own
