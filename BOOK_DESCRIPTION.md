## Full Description (Amazon listing)

**He came to investigate a murder. He never expected to become the suspect.**

True crime podcaster Josh Caine arrives at remote Thornewood Estate to research an unsolved killing for his next book. The isolated Oregon mansion has a dark history—a string of deaths going back four decades, including a young woman murdered in 2020. It's exactly the kind of story that built his career.

But something about the estate's groundskeeper doesn't add up.

Eleanor is beautiful, guarded, and far too interested in Josh's work. She watches him when she thinks he isn't looking. She asks questions that cut too close. And despite every instinct screaming that she's dangerous, Josh can't stay away from her.

When a winter storm traps them together for months, the tension between suspicion and desire becomes unbearable. Josh starts waking with no memory of the night before, finding evidence he can't explain. He's certain Eleanor is drugging him, setting him up for something. But the closer he gets to the truth, the more he realizes that the real danger at Thornewood might not be her at all.

Some secrets are buried for a reason. Some truths will destroy everything.

**A dark, twisting psychological thriller with explicit romance. For readers who like their love stories dangerous and their plot twists devastating.**
