## Remaining observations (NOT recommended for fix this session)

These did not reach the threshold for proposed edits, but noting them so future audits can decide:

1. **Ch 20 L6453** — "Everything I'd collected over the months. All the leverage I'd been assembling." "Leverage" is mildly investigator-adjacent. In predator context it reads as personal leverage (what I know about him), so it's ambiguous enough to survive. Flagging as LOW/monitor only.

2. **"Finish" / "finish what I started" / "finish this"** — appears 20+ times in Eleanor POV across Chapters 1-24. I chose not to propose changes because (a) the word is ambiguous between predator and investigator readings, (b) it's a book-wide pattern that appears deliberate, and (c) changing it would be invasive. If a future reviewer disagrees, the simplest one-word swap is "finish" → "end" in 3-5 strategic spots.

3. **Eleanor's pill-crushing scenes** (L605 Ch 3, L915 Ch 4, L1910 Ch 7, L3449 Ch 11) — the density is controlled (1/chapter), explanations (migraine meds, stress) are well-seeded, and the symptoms post-drugging Josh are cleanly attributed to drugging, not illness. CLEAN.

4. **Ch 22 L6643 Josh POV** — "I kept thinking about the way Eleanor had looked at my mother's photo. That moment of stillness when I'd shown it to her." Josh noticing Eleanor's reaction isn't leaky on its own. Previously the danger came from the immediate Eleanor-POV follow-up at L6678 ("weapon"); with that fixed, this Josh observation now reads as Josh's general paranoia rather than as setup for a specific payoff. CLEAN.

5. **Ch 24 L7382 / L7710 still use "loft"** — this is now consistent with Eleanor's established spatial language (her locked loft room). If you ever want to use a different word to further distance from "evidence room" semantics, "locked room" also works, but "loft" already appears several times as Eleanor's secret space and reads naturally.
