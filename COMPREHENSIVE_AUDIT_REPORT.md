### FIX 1: Father as Killer Reveal (CRITICAL) ✅ FIXED
**Location:** Chapter 27, line 9219
**Problem:** The Story Bible specifies Josh's FATHER killed his mother, but this was never explicitly stated in the reveal scene. Eleanor only said Josh "witnessed something" without identifying the killer.
**Fix Applied:** Added explicit revelation: "Your mother was murdered at Thornewood, Josh. By your father." and "You saw it happen—saw him kill her. The police could never prove it; he disappeared after that night, was never found."

### FIX 2: Neurological Symptom Too Clinical (HIGH) ✅ FIXED
**Location:** Chapter 18, line 5723
**Problem:** "My left hand stopped working" was too clinically specific, potentially tipping off medical-savvy readers to Eleanor's brain tumor.
**Fix Applied:** Changed to "My grip just... failed. Fingers going slack without warning." This sounds more like situational stress than neurological diagnosis.
