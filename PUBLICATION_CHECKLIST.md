### Kindle Unlimited - DECISION NEEDED
- [ ] Enroll in KDP Select? [ ] Yes [ ] No [ ] Undecided

**KDP Select Pros:**
- Access to Kindle Unlimited readers (huge romance/thriller audience)
- Page-read royalties (~$0.004-0.005 per page)
- Promotional tools (Kindle Countdown, Free Book promotions)
- Most dark romance/romantic suspense readers are on KU

**KDP Select Cons:**
- 90-day exclusivity (cannot sell on Apple, Kobo, Google Play, etc.)
- Must renew or opt-out every 90 days
- Locked into Amazon ecosystem

**Recommendation for your genre:** YES - romantic suspense/dark romance readers heavily use Kindle Unlimited
